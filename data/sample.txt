The fictional Republic of Norland held a national election in 2024. 
The election took place on October 12, 2024, and had three major political parties:
the National Reform Party (NRP), the Democratic Alliance (DA), and the Green Future Party (GFP).

The National Reform Party won 48% of the popular vote and secured 142 seats in the 
300-seat parliament. The Democratic Alliance received 41% of the vote and won 121 seats.
The Green Future Party received 11% of the vote and won 37 seats.

Because no party won a majority of the 300 parliamentary seats, the National Reform 
Party entered coalition negotiations with the Green Future Party. On October 28, 
2024, the two parties announced a coalition agreement.

Under the agreement, the leader of the National Reform Party, Elena Varga, became 
Prime Minister. The leader of the Green Future Party, Tomas Ren, became Deputy Prime 
Minister and Minister for Environmental Affairs.

The coalition government identified three major priorities: expanding renewable 
energy production, modernizing the country's railway network, and reducing the 
government budget deficit.

In its first 100 days, the government announced a national railway modernization 
program with an estimated cost of 18 billion Norland crowns. The government also 
announced a target of generating 45% of the country's electricity from renewable 
sources by 2030.

The opposition Democratic Alliance criticized the railway program, arguing that the 
government had not provided enough information about how the project would be funded.
The government responded that the program would be financed through a combination of 
government spending, infrastructure bonds, and private-sector investment.

The fictional Republic of Norland uses a parliamentary system. The Prime Minister is 
the head of government, while the President has primarily ceremonial responsibilities.
The parliament has 300 seats, and a party or coalition needs at least 151 seats to 
hold a majority.
